# Information Sources (PRISMA-ScR)

## Databases

1. **PubMed / MEDLINE:** Search for "National Health Reform Agreement" AND ("Funding" OR "Safety" OR "Quality").
2. **Scopus:** Broad health sciences coverage.
3. **Google Scholar:** For citation chaining and grey literature.

## Grey Literature / Policy Repositories

1. **Federal Register of Legislation:** For primary NHRA texts and amendments.
2. **IHACPA (Independent Health and Aged Care Pricing Authority):** Determination reports and pricing frameworks.
