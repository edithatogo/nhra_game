# Eligibility Criteria (PRISMA-ScR)
**Author:** Dylan A Mordaunt

## Inclusion Criteria
1.  **Population:** Australian Health System, Public Hospitals, Federal and State/Territory Health Departments.
2.  **Concept:**
    *   National Health Reform Agreement (NHRA).
    *   Activity Based Funding (ABF) incentives and behaviors.
    *   **"Strategic Gaming"** or gaming of safety/quality metrics (e.g., HACs, NEPs).
    *   Structural analysis of the Agreement's clauses.
3.  **Context:** The era of the NHRA (2011–Present).
4.  **Evidence Types:**
    *   Legal texts (NHRA and Addendums).
    *   Government reports (AIHW, IHACPA, Administrator of the National Health Funding Pool).
    *   Academic literature (Health Policy, Health Economics, Law).
    *   Grey literature (Think tank reports, Union position statements).

## Exclusion Criteria
1.  **Clinical Focus:** Studies focusing solely on clinical efficacy of interventions without systemic funding implications.
2.  **Geography:** Non-Australian jurisdictions (unless used as a direct comparative comparator).
3.  **Timeframe:** Pre-2011 funding models (e.g., historical block grants) except as background context.
4.  **Language:** Non-English documents.
