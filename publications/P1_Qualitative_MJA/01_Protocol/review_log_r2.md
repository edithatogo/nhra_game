## Internal Feedback Summary

### 1. Safety & Reputation
*   **Critique:** Payoffs are currently too narrow (financial only).
*   **Suggestion:** Add "Reputational Payoffs" (e.g., performance ranking visibility) as a key utility for LHNs.
*   **Technical Note:** Note the "PSI-90" as a noisy indicator of true state.

### 2. Legal Nuance
*   **Critique:** "Moves" are treated as atomic.
*   **Suggestion:** Account for "Iterative moves" in data quality cycles. Clause 127-130 of the 2020 Addendum defines a back-and-forth process.

### 3. Sociological Grounding
*   **Critique:** Reflexivity is still thin on the *why* of the gaming behaviors.
*   **Suggestion:** Connect the "Strategic Gaming" construct to "Institutional Isomorphism" (DiMaggio & Powell). The protocol should look for clauses that force organizations to adopt similar symbolic masks, regardless of local clinical reality.

### 4. Mathematical Precision
*   **Suggestion:** Formalize the uncertainty as "Strategic Uncertainty" (what will the other player do?) vs "Stochastic Uncertainty" (natural variation in data).

## Final Implementation Plan
1.  **Refine Payoffs:** Explicitly include reputation/ranking in utility functions.
2.  **Sociological Linkage:** Add a sentence in Background linking to Institutional Isomorphism.
3.  **Iterative Moves:** Acknowledge the "Negotiation Loop" in data submission.
