### 2. Legal Nuance (DN)
*   **Critique:** "Moves" are treated as atomic.
*   **Suggestion:** Account for "Iterative moves" in data quality cycles. Clause 127-130 of the 2020 Addendum defines a back-and-forth process.

### 3. Sociological Grounding (FG)
*   **Critique:** Reflexivity is still thin on the *why* of the theatre.
*   **Suggestion:** Connect the "Safety Theatre" construct to "Institutional Isomorphism" (DiMaggio & Powell). The protocol should look for clauses that force LHNs to look the same, regardless of local clinical reality.

### 4. Mathematical Precision (MT)
*   **Suggestion:** Formalize the uncertainty as "Strategic Uncertainty" (what will the other player do?) vs "Stochastic Uncertainty" (natural variation in data).
