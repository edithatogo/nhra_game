# Appendix A — IHACPA Pricing Framework: Per‑year methodological change summaries

Compiled from IHACPA Pricing Framework documents, NEP/NEC determinations, and consultation reports as listed in `sources.md` (retrieval date: 2025-12-27). Each entry below summarises the most salient timing or methodological change recorded in the public materials for that year.

- **2012–13:** Earliest Pricing Framework in the available series; establishes baseline ABF parameters and the NEP/NEC determination process that subsequent frameworks reference.

- **2013–14:** Iterative refinement of definitions and schedule items from the baseline year; no major structural changes recorded in the public materials.

- **2014–15:** Notable timing change — the framework was published prior to the NEP/NEC determinations to increase transparency and provide consultation feedback earlier in the cycle.

- **2015–16:** Incremental clarifications to costing methodology and schedule; maintained continuity with prior-year rule statements.

- **2016–17:** Continued stability of rule constructs with targeted updates to technical appendices (costing and classification notes).

- **2017–18:** Minor amendments and editorial clarifications; no abrupt methodological regime change identified.

- **2018–19:** Another year where the framework was published ahead of NEP/NEC to prioritise transparency and to capture consultation input earlier in the process.

- **2019–20:** Builds on prior years; public NEP/NEC determinations were documented around Mar 2019/2020 and provide explicit signals used in later performance analyses.

- **2020–21:** Schedule and consultation processes adjusted to align with the NHRA Addendum 2020–25 (noted changes to consultation timing and some definitional clarifications).

- **2021–22:** Framework published Feb 2022; schedule and consultation timing were modified to accommodate ongoing Addendum consultations.

- **2022–23:** Framework published Aug 2022; documented methodology updates and an accompanying consultation report provide useful detail on measurement and reporting changes.

- **2023–24:** Framework published Dec 2022; published ahead of NEP/NEC to provide transparency and capture consultation feedback, with an emphasis on publishing technical appendices.

- **2024–25:** Framework published Dec 2023; emphasis on transparency continued and technical appendices were expanded to provide more replicable methodological detail.

- **2025–26:** Framework published Dec 2024; associated NEP/NEC determinations published Mar 2025 and a consultation report is available documenting the consultation inputs and responses.

- **2026–27:** Framework published Dec 2025; consultation activity occurred May–Jun 2025 and NEP/NEC determinations were pending as of Mar 2026 (see `sources.md` for updates on NEC/NEP publication dates).
