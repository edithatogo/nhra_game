# OSF Preregistration: NHRA Structural Game Theory Analysis

**Author:** Dylan A Mordaunt

## 1. Study Information

* **Title:** Structural Analysis of the National Health Reform Agreement using Game Theory
* **Description:** This study provides a rigorous qualitative mapping of the legal and statutory text of the National Health Reform Agreement (NHRA) 2011 and its subsequent Addendums (2017, 2020-2025). The objective is to identify the structural vulnerabilities and "Constructive Ambiguity" within the Agreement that may create mathematical incentives for **Strategic Gaming** (the optimization of metric reporting over substantive clinical quality).
* **Hypothesis:** The current NHRA incentive structure, characterized by imperfect information and multi-layered agency (Federal-State-LHN), mathematically favors symbolic compliance and performative reporting over genuine system performance improvements. We hypothesize the existence of specific "Fragility Nodes" where current audit pressures are insufficient to deter gaming behaviors.

## 2. Design Plan

* **Study Type:** Qualitative Document Analysis with Game Theoretic Formalization.
* **Blinding:** A dual-pass "Clean Room" coding protocol is utilized. Two divergent analytical lenses (Rule-Strict vs. Context-Applied) independently code the source material to ensure inter-rater reliability and capture the gap between formal statutory rules and operational "Rules-in-Use."
* **Study Design:** The study utilizes an **Extensive Form Game with Imperfect Information** framework. This design models the sequential interactions between the Commonwealth (Principal), States (Agents), and Local Health Networks (Sub-agents), accounting for the information asymmetry and time lags inherent in the national health data cycle.

## 3. Sampling Plan

* **Existing Data:** Yes (Statutory and Policy texts).
* **Explanation:** The study analyzes the publicly available census of National Health Reform documents from 2011 to 2025.
* **Data Collection Procedures:** Coding is performed using the Institutional Analysis and Development (IAD) framework. Source documents include the NHRA 2011, the National Health Reform Act 2011 (Cth), the 2017 and 2020-2025 Addendums, and IHACPA Pricing Frameworks.

## 4. Variables

* **Measured Variables:**
  * *Players:* Identifying decision-making entities and their utility functions.
  * *Moves:* Mapping the set of legally available actions (e.g., funding determination, data submission, audit initiation).
  * *Information Sets:* Defining what players know at each node (e.g., contemporaneous performance vs. lagged audited reports).
  * *Payoffs:* Quantifying both financial transfers (NWAU revenue) and reputational standing (Public performance signals).

## 5. Analysis Plan

* **Statistical Models:** Game theoretic Nash Equilibrium and Subgame Perfect Equilibrium analysis.
* **Reconciliation:** Analysis of blinded coding discrepancies followed by systematic arbitration based on the Statutory Lens.
* **Validation:** Logical completeness testing of the resulting game trees and sensitivity analysis of payoff weighting parameters.

## 6. Other

* **Reflexivity:** Utilizes structured analytical lenses to simulate divergent perspectives, mitigating the model bias inherent in automated qualitative synthesis.
* **Reporting:** Standards for Reporting Qualitative Research (SRQR) and PRISMA-ScR for supplemental literature identification.
