# OSF Preregistration: NHRA Structural Game Theory Analysis
**Author:** Dylan A Mordaunt

## 1. Study Information
*   **Title:** Structural Analysis of the National Health Reform Agreement using Game Theory
*   **Description:** A qualitative mapping of the NHRA legal text to identifying structural incentives for 'Safety Theatre'.
*   **Hypothesis:** The current NHRA incentive structure mathematically favors symbolic reporting over substantive safety improvements.

## 2. Design Plan
*   **Study Type:** Qualitative Document Analysis.
*   **Blinding:** Two independent coders (Methodologist vs. Policy Expert).
*   **Study Design:** Extensive Form Game with Imperfect Information.

## 3. Sampling Plan
*   **Existing Data:** Yes (Legal texts).
*   **Explanation:** Analyzing the publicly available NHRA 2011 and Addendums.
*   **Data Collection Procedures:** Clean Room coding of statutory and policy documents.

## 4. Variables
*   **Measured Variables:** Players, Moves, Information Sets, Payoffs (Financial/Reputational).

## 5. Analysis Plan
*   **Statistical Models:** Game theoretic equilibrium analysis.
*   **Reconciliation:** Blinded coding comparison with legal arbitration.

## 6. Other
*   **Reflexivity:** Utilizes simulated expert personas to mitigate bias.
*   **Reporting:** Standards for Reporting Qualitative Research (SRQR).
