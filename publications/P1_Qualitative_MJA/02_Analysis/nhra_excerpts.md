# NHRA Statutory Excerpts (Clean Room Source)

## Funding and Activity Based Funding (ABF)
*   **Clause A127 (2020 Addendum):** "A single National Health Funding Pool will be maintained, comprising a Reserve Bank of Australia account for each State, for the purposes of receiving all Commonwealth and activity-based State public hospital funding."
*   **National Efficient Price (NEP):** Defined annually by IHACPA to compute efficient payments based on NWAU weights.

## Safety and Quality (HACs)
*   **Clauses A161-171 (2020 Addendum):** Address safety and quality measures, including Hospital Acquired Complications (HACs).
*   **HAC Definition:** A complication that arises during a hospital stay where clinical risk mitigation strategies can reduce risk.
*   **HAC Funding Adjustment:** Reduces funding for admitted acute care episodes where a HAC is identified, reflecting incremental cost.
*   **Risk Adjustment:** Complexity scores (low, medium, high) adjust the magnitude of the funding reduction.
*   **Rule of Max:** If multiple HACs occur, the one with the largest adjustment determines the reduction.

## Data and Reporting
*   **IHACPA Role:** Responsible for data quality assurance and setting the rules for NWAU calculation.
*   **Signals:** Performance rankings based on PSI-90 and other safety/quality indicators.
