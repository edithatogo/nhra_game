# Cover Letter: P1 Qualitative Mapping Study

To: Editor-in-Chief, Medical Journal of Australia

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit the manuscript titled **"Structural Analysis of the National Health Reform Agreement: A Game Theoretic Mapping of Strategic Gaming Incentives"** for consideration as an Original Research article in the *Medical Journal of Australia*.

This study addresses a critical gap in the Australian health policy landscape: the structural drivers of strategic behavior under Activity Based Funding. While "gaming" is frequently discussed in clinical and administrative circles, it has rarely been subjected to formal game-theoretic analysis. By mapping the NHRA statutory text to an extensive-form game framework, we identify specific "fragility nodes" where the current incentive structure mathematically favors symbolic compliance over substantive safety improvements.

We believe our findings regarding "Strategic Gaming" and "Institutional Isomorphism" will be of significant interest to your readership, particularly medical administrators and policy-makers involved in the ongoing NHRA negotiations.

This manuscript is original work and has not been submitted elsewhere. All authors have approved the final version.

Sincerely,

Dylan A Mordaunt
Flinders University
 Adelaide, Australia
