# Analytical Lenses for Single-Author Review
**Lead Author:** Dylan A Mordaunt

To ensure methodological rigour and multi-disciplinary validity in the absence of a large external panel, this project utilizes a structured single-author review protocol. This involves systematic evaluation of all documents through six distinct analytical lenses:

## 1. Methodological Rigour Lens
*   **Focus:** Compliance with reporting standards (SRQR, PRISMA, ODD).
*   **Check:** Inter-rater reliability simulations and coding framework stability.

## 2. Policy & Operational Context Lens
*   **Focus:** Real-world alignment with the National Health Reform Agreement (NHRA).
*   **Check:** Accuracy of funding mechanisms (ABF, NEP) and inter-governmental dynamics.

## 3. Game Theoretic Logic Lens
*   **Focus:** Mathematical consistency of the stage games.
*   **Check:** Equilibrium selection rules, payoff matrix stability, and extensive form tree completeness.

## 4. Clinical Executive Lens
*   **Focus:** Practical implications for hospital administration and Local Health Networks (LHNs).
*   **Check:** Validity of performance metrics (PSI-90, HACs) and reputational utility.

## 5. Legal & Statutory Lens
*   **Focus:** Strict statutory interpretation.
*   **Check:** Alignment between the *National Health Reform Act 2011* (Cth) and the Agreement text.

## 6. Organizational Sociology Lens
*   **Focus:** Institutional drivers of strategic behavior.
*   **Check:** Grounding of "Strategic Gaming" in theories of Decoupling and Institutional Isomorphism.
