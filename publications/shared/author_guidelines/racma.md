# RACMA Position Statement Standards (Inferred)

## Structure

1. **Title:** Clear, directive (e.g., "Position Statement on X").
2. **Executive Summary / Key Position:** 1-2 paragraphs stating the College's stance unequivocally.
3. **Background / Context:** Evidence base, current landscape, why this matters now.
4. **Key Issues:** Bulleted breakdown of the problem.
5. **Recommendations / Call to Action:** Specific, actionable steps for Government/Health Services.
6. **References:** Key evidence only (Vancouver style).

## Style

* **Tone:** Authoritative, professional, advocacy-oriented but evidence-based.
* **Audience:** Government Ministers, Policy Makers, Health Executives.
* **Length:** 2-4 pages standard (approx. 1000-1500 words).
* **Reading Level:** Grade 10-12 (Plain English).
