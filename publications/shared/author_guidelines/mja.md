# MJA Author Guidelines Summary

## Research Articles
*   **Word Count:** 3000 words max (Main text).
*   **Abstract:** 300 words max, Structured (Objectives, Design, Setting, Participants, Interventions, Main outcome measures, Results, Conclusions).
*   **References:** 40 max (Strict).
*   **Tables/Figures:** 5-6 max total.
*   **Structure:** Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion.
*   **Style:** Active voice preferred where appropriate, clear humanistic language, minimal jargon.
*   **Formatting:** Times New Roman 12pt, 1.5 spacing (for submission).

## Perspectives / Policy
*   **Word Count:** 1500-2000 words (Estimated).
*   **Abstract:** Unstructured, ~150-200 words.
*   **References:** ~20-30 max.
*   **Structure:** Argument-driven (Problem, Evidence, Policy Implication).

## General
*   **Citations:** Superscript numbers in text. Bibliography at end (Vancouver/Numbered style).
*   **Blinding:** Double-anonymous peer review (Title page separate).
