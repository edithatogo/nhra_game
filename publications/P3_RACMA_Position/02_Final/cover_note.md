# RACMA Position Statement: Fix the NHRA
**Lead Author:** Dylan A Mordaunt

## Overview
This position statement outlines RACMA's formal stance on the structural reform of the National Health Reform Agreement (NHRA). It is supported by a comprehensive series of qualitative and quantitative studies identifying the drivers of "Strategic Gaming" in the Australian public hospital system.

## Key Recommendations
1.  **Transparency Surges:** Real-time data publication to narrow information asymmetry.
2.  **Cap Reform:** Aligning the 6.5% Commonwealth growth cap with actual input costs.
3.  **Governance Shift:** Moving from performative documentation to High Reliability principles.

## Target Audience
- RACMA Board and Fellowship.
- Commonwealth and State Health Ministers.
- National Health Funding Pool stakeholders.
