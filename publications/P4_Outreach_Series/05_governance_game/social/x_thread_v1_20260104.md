1/ Who is actually in charge of a public hospital? 🏛️🏥

The Ministry? Or the Local Network?

It’s a tug-of-war we call the **Governance Game**. 🧵

2/ The Players:
🏢 **Principal (Ministry)**: Wants control, safety, consistency.
🏥 **Agent (LHN)**: Wants autonomy, speed, local responsiveness.

3/ The Payoffs:

Option A: **Integrate (Centralise)**.
High control, but the LHN loses agency. "I just work here." 😐

Option B: **Separate (Devolve)**.
High autonomy, but the Ministry loses visibility. "What are they doing over there?" 🫣

4/ The Nash Equilibrium is often **Separate**.

LHNs will fight for independence.
Ministries eventually realize they can't run the ED roster from Head Office.

But without trust, this separation is hostile. Constant audits, unexpected directives, hidden deficits. 📉

5/ The Solution? **Earned Autonomy.**

You don't get autonomy for free. You earn it with performance.
And the Ministry doesn't get control for free. They earn it by providing support, not just red tape.

6/ Key enabler: **Data Transparency.**

If the Ministry can see the dashboard in real-time, they don't need to send an email asking for a report.
Visibility replaces Control. 👁️📊
