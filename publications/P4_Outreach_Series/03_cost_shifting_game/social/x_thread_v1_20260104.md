1/ Ever feel like the health system is just passing the buck? 💸🏥

In Australia, the "buck" often moves between the Commonwealth and the States.

We call this the **NHRA Cost Shifting Game**.

Here is why it happens and why patients feel it. #HealthPolicy #NHRA

2/ Australia's health system has a split budget.
🔹 Commonwealth: GPs, primary care, aged care.
🔹 States: Public hospitals, EDs.

When these budgets are separate, an "improvement" in one can be a "burden" for the other. 📉➡️📈

3/ Example A: If primary care access drops (Federal saving), ED visits spike (State cost). 🚑

Example B: If hospital discharge happens too fast (State saving), readmissions or aged care needs rise (Federal cost). 🏥🔁

4/ We model this using game theory.

Posture 1: **Invest**. Spend now to fix the root cause.
Posture 2: **Shift**. Save your own budget and shunt the problem to the other side.

Guess which one is the Nash equilibrium? (Hint: it’s the bad one). 🤡

5/ Why is "Shift" so sticky?

If you Invest and the other side Shifts, you get hit twice. You pay for the fix AND you still inherit their burden.

Economists call this a **negative externality**. 🧠📊

6/ The result? "System Failure / Low Cost."

Each side "saves" on paper, but the system as a whole becomes more expensive and less effective. Patients get stuck in the middle of budget wars. 🛑🧍‍♂️🧍‍♀️

7/ How to break the game?
✅ Pooled funding models.
✅ Outcome-based signals (patient journey).
✅ Transparent data (IHACPA/AIHW) to track cost migration.
