# Health funding: Why are we just moving problems around? 🏥💸

Ever wonder why it's so hard to get a GP appointment, yet hospital EDs are overflowing?

It’s not just "bad luck." It’s a game of incentives.

In Australia, the **National Health Reform Agreement (NHRA)** sets the rules for how our hospitals are funded. But because the Federal and State governments pay for different parts of the system, we often see a **Cost Shifting Game**.

🔴 **Federal saves** on GPs → **State pays** for ED visits.
🔴 **State saves** on early discharge → **Federal pays** for aged care or GP follow-ups.

When we model this using game theory, we find that shifting costs is often the "rational" move for each budget-holder, even if it’s bad for the patient and more expensive for the taxpayer overall.

How do we fix it?
1️⃣ Move toward **pooled funding** where incentives align.
2️⃣ Measure **patient outcomes** across the whole journey, not just activity.
3️⃣ Use **transparent data** to show where costs are moving.
