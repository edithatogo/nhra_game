# Health funding: Why are we just moving problems around? 🏥💸

Ever wonder why it's so hard to get a GP appointment, yet hospital EDs are overflowing?

It’s not just "bad luck." It’s a game of incentives.

Australia's health system has a split brain:
🔴 **Federal saves** on GPs → **State pays** for ED visits.
🔴 **State saves** on early discharge → **Federal pays** for follow-up care.

We call this the **Cost Shifting Game**.

When we model this, we find that shifting costs is often the "rational" move for a budget-holder—even if it makes the whole system more expensive and hurts patients.

To fix it, we need:
1️⃣ **Pooled funding** (aligning the buckets).
2️⃣ **Patient-centred metrics** (tracking the journey, not the building).
3️⃣ **Data transparency** (seeing where the cost goes).
