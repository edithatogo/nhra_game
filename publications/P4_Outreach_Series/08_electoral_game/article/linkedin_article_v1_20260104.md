# The "Electoral Game": why health funding goes boom and bust

Why do we see massive health spending announcements before an election, followed by "efficiency dividends" afterwards?

It’s the **Electoral Game**.

Health is a "high salience" issue—voters care about it deeply.
But it is also expensive.

## The real-world situation

- **The Incumbent (Government)** wants to be re-elected. They need to show they "care" (Salience) but also that they are "responsible" (Fiscal).
- **The Opposition** wants to win. They attack on whichever flank is weak.

## The "game" incentives

We model this as a Salience War.

- **Salience Strategy**: "We are spending BILLIONS!" (Wins votes, hurts budget).
- **Fiscal Strategy**: "We are managing the economy." (Saves budget, risks votes).

If the Government cuts spending (Fiscal), the Opposition attacks on "Health Crisis" (Salience).
If the Government spends huge (Salience), the Opposition attacks on "Waste/Debt" (Fiscal).

## The Equilibrium: "Cyclical Salience"

There is no stable point. The system cycles.
1.  **Election Mode**: Spend, spend, spend! (Boost Salience).
2.  **Post-Election**: "Oh no, the deficit!" (Cut spending).
3.  **Mid-Term**: Wait lists blow out. Opposition attacks.
4.  **Pre-Election**: Panic spend! (Repeat).

## How to fix it

- **Independent Funding Authority**: Like the Reserve Bank, but for health funding. Remove the political cycle.
- **Multi-year agreements**: Lock in funding for 5-10 years, not 3-year election cycles.
- **Outcome reporting**: Force the debate to be about "health gained," not just "dollars spent."

## Evidence / further reading

- AES Election Study 2022.
- Duckett, S. (2022) *The Australian Health Care System*.

## TL;DR

- Health funding follows the election cycle, not the patient need cycle.
- Governments oscillate between "Salience" (Spending) and "Responsibility" (Cutting).
- Independent funding bodies can smooth the boom-bust cycle.
