1/ Why does health funding feel like a rollercoaster? 🎢🏥

Boom before an election. Bust afterwards.

It’s the **Electoral Game**. 🧵

2/ The Drivers:
🗳️ **Salience**: "Do voters care?" (Yes, health is #1).
💰 **Fiscal**: "Can we afford it?" (No, it's expensive).

3/ The Cycle:

Phase 1: **The Campaign**.
Incumbent needs votes.
Strategy: "Record Spending!"
Result: Budget blowout, but you win. ✅

Phase 2: ** The Hangover**.
Election over. Treasury looks at the books.
Strategy: "Efficiency Dividend."
Result: Budget repaired, but wait lists grow. 📉

Phase 3: **The Attack**.
Opposition sees the wait lists.
Strategy: "Health Crisis!"
Incumbent panics.
Go to Phase 1. 🔁

4/ This cycle destroys long-term planning.
You can't fix workforce shortages or chronic disease in a 3-year window.

Hospitals get "sugar hits" of cash, then starvation diets.

5/ The Equilibrium: "Salience Wars."
Policy is driven by headlines, not health outcomes.

6/ The Fix? **Depoliticise Funding.**
We have an independent Reserve Bank for interest rates.
Why not an independent Commission for health funding?

Set the price based on need, not polls. 📉➡️🛑
