# Why health funding goes BOOM then BUST 🗳️📉

Election year? "Record health spending!" 💸
Year after? "Efficiency dividend." ✂️

This isn't an accident. It's the **Electoral Game**.

Health is a "high salience" issue. Voters care.
But it's also expensive.

So we see a cycle:
1️⃣ **Pre-Election**: Spend to win votes.
2️⃣ **Post-Election**: Cut to fix the budget.
3️⃣ **Repeat**.

This cycle makes long-term reform impossible. You can't build a 10-year workforce plan on a 3-year political cycle.

The fix? **Independent Funding**.
Take the credit card away from the politicians and give it to an independent body (like the RBA).
