Tweet 1: Ever watched people argue over *definitions* and thought “this is pointless”? In NHRA funding, definitions can decide who pays, what gets counted, and what gets squeezed.

Tweet 2: Two incentives collide: the Commonwealth wants clear, containable rules; States want rules that match messy hospital reality under pressure.

Tweet 3: You can model this as a tiny 2×2 “definition game”: Realism vs Strictness. Each side picks a posture that best protects their goals.

Tweet 4: The “equilibrium” isn’t what’s best — it’s what tends to stick because neither side can improve by changing alone. Tight definitions can be sticky… until pressure makes the consequences visible.
