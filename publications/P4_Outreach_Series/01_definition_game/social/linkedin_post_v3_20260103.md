If you think arguments about “definitions” are boring, you’ve never seen one decide who pays billions.

Under the NHRA, what *counts* as an in-scope “hospital service” shapes funding, behaviour, and (eventually) pressure on the system.

Here’s the game-theory punchline: when incentives don’t line up, “strict definitions” can become a sticky default… until real-world pressure makes the consequences impossible to ignore.
