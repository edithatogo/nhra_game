1/ Why do hospitals get "bed blocked"? 🚑🛑

It’s not just lack of beds. It’s a **Coordination Game** gone wrong.

Here is the Game Theory of discharge delays. 🧵

2/ The Players:
🏥 **Acute Hospital** (Needs bed for new patient).
🏡 **Downstream** (Aged Care / NDIS / GP).

Both want the patient well. But their incentives clash.

3/ The Payoff Matrix:

Option A: **Coordinate**. High effort from both. Patient flows. 🌊
Option B: **Fragment**. Protect your own resources. Patient waits. 🧱

4/ If I Coordinate (Push) but you Fragment (Block), I burn out my staff for no result. "Sucker's Payoff."

So the safe move is to Fragment.
"We'll wait until they are 100% ready."
"We'll wait until a spot is 100% free."

5/ Result: **Gridlock Equilibrium.**

Everyone waits.
The patient is stuck in an expensive acute bed ($2k/day) instead of a cheaper community spot ($300/day).

Inefficient AND bad for the patient. 📉

6/ Fixing a Coordination Game requires **Trust** or **Mechanism Design.**

You can't just shout "Work Harder!"

Mechanisms:
✅ Transitional Care (Step-down beds).
✅ Joint Discharge Teams (Shared brain).
✅ Pooled Budgets (Shared wallet).
