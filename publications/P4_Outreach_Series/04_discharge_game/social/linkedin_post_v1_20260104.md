# Why bed block happens (even when everyone hates it) 🚑🛌

Bed block is the biggest bottleneck in our hospitals.
Patients stuck in ambulances. EDs overflowing.

It’s a classic **Coordination Failure**.

In our latest Game Theory model, we look at the standoff between **Acute Care** and **Aged Care/NDIS**.

Each side has an incentive to protect its own resources.
If I push and you block, I waste my time.
If you pull and I delay, you waste a spot.

So the "safe" strategy? **Wait.**
Result: **Gridlock**.

A patient sits in a $2,000/day acute bed waiting for a $300/day community spot. 💸

Fixing this isn't just about money—it's about **trust mechanisms**:
✅ Discharge Liaisons.
✅ Step-down capacity.
✅ Shared KPIs.
