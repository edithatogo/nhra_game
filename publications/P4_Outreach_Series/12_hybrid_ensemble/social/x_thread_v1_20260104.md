1/ Why is the health crisis so hard to fix? 🧩🏥

Because it’s not one problem. It’s a **Network of Games**.

Here is how our Hybrid Model explains the mess. 🧵

2/ We usually look at problems in isolation.
"Ambulance Ramping" -> "Buy more ambulances." 🚑
"Bed Block" -> "Buy more beds." 🛏️

But this ignores the connections.

3/ The Chain Reaction:
1. **Federal** freezes GP rebates (Stackelberg Game).
2. Patients flock to **ED** (Queuing Game).
3. ED gets full. **Hospital** tries to discharge patients to Aged Care.
4. Aged Care says "Not ready" (Discharge Game). 🛑

4/ Meanwhile:
5. **State** is running a deficit (Internal Competition Game).
6. **Government** is in "Budget Repair" mode post-election (Electoral Game).

Result? Gridlock. Everywhere. 📉

5/ This is **Systemic Drag**.
Friction in one layer multiplies as it moves through the system.
A small cut upstream causes a massive blowout downstream.

6/ The Lesson:
Single-point solutions fail.
"More beds" fill up instantly if the inflow is broken.
"More GPs" fail if the discharge path is blocked.

7/ We need **Holistic Reform**.
Aligned incentives across the ENTIRE journey.
Pooled funding. Shared data. Joint governance.
