1/ Why does the ED wait time never seem to drop, even when we add beds? 🚑⏱️

It’s the **Queuing Equilibrium**.
And it’s the same math that explains traffic jams. 🚗🚙

Here is why. 🧵

2/ The Choice:
A patient needs help.
Option A: GP. 🩺
Cost: $50 gap.
Wait: 3 days.

Option B: ED. 🏥
Cost: $0.
Wait: 4 hours.

3/ The Incentive:
If the GP is expensive/unavailable, the ED is the rational choice.
Even with the wait.

4/ The Feedback Loop:
Suppose we add 50 beds to the ED.
Efficiency goes up. Wait time drops to 1 hour.

Now Option B is MUCH better.
Cost: $0. Wait: 1 hour.

Result? Patients who were tolerating the GP now switch to the ED. 🏃‍♂️

5/ This is **Induced Demand.**
The extra patients fill the new beds.
The wait time creeps back up to 4 hours.
Equilibrium is restored. ⚖️

6/ The Lesson:
"Wait Time" acts as a price.
If the monetary price is $0, the time price will rise until demand matches supply.

You cannot "build your way out" of this if the alternative (Primary Care) is broken. 🛑

7/ The Fix?
Make the Alternative better.
✅ Urgent Care Clinics (Free, Fast).
✅ Better GP Bulk Billing.
✅ Virtual ED.

Shift the demand curve, don't just expand the supply curve.
