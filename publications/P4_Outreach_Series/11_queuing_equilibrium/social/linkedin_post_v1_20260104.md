# Why adding hospital beds doesn't always fix the wait list 🚑📉

It’s called **Induced Demand**.
Just like building more lanes on a highway makes more people drive. 🚗

In the NHRA, we model this as a **Queuing Equilibrium**.

Patients choose between GP (Cost $, Wait Days) and ED (Free, Wait Hours).
If you make the ED faster/better, it becomes MORE attractive than the GP.
So more people come.
And the wait time goes back up. 📈

We are effectively using "waiting" as a rationing tool. That's tragic.

The only way to fix the ED is to fix the **Alternative**.
Make Primary Care cheaper and faster. 📉
