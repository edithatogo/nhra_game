# Negotiation 101: Patience pays ⏳💰

Why do some health deals favor the Commonwealth over the States?

It’s often about **Time**.

In Game Theory, the **Rubinstein Bargaining** model proves that the "share of the pie" goes to the most patient player.

If you are desperate for cash (High Discount Rate), you accept a bad deal today to avoid waiting for a good deal tomorrow.
If you have a surplus, you can wait.

In the NHRA, fiscal stress makes States impatient.
This structurally weakens their bargaining position. 📉

To fix federalism, States need **fiscal buffers**—the ability to say "No" and wait.
