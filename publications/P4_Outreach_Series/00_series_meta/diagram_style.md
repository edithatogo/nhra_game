# Diagram Style Guide

## Goals

- Mobile-legible
- Colour-blind-safe
- Consistent across the series

## Defaults

- Prefer simple shapes and short labels.
- Avoid red/green-only encoding; use patterns or multiple channels.
- Use consistent naming for actors: Commonwealth, State, LHN, Provider, Auditor, Patients.

## Outputs

- Always export a master `.svg` plus exact-dimension `.png` targets for LinkedIn.
