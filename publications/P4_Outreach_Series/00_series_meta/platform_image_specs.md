# Platform Image Specs (LinkedIn)

Note: Platform requirements can change. This file documents the targets used by this repository’s rendering/validation scripts.

## Targets

### LinkedIn article cover (landscape)

- 1x: 1200 × 644 px (PNG)
- 2x: 2400 × 1288 px (PNG)

### LinkedIn feed post image (square)

- 1x: 1200 × 1200 px (PNG)
- 2x: 2400 × 2400 px (PNG)

## Safe-area guidance

- Keep title text away from edges to tolerate mobile cropping.
- Ensure legibility at phone width; prefer large type and low detail.
