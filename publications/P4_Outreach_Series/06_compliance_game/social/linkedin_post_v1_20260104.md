# The Cat-and-Mouse Game of Hospital Funding 😼🐭

If a hospital gets paid more for a "complex" patient, what happens?
They find ways to make patients look complex.

This is "Upcoding," and it sparks the **Compliance Game**.

In our latest Game Theory model, we look at the cycle between **Auditors** and **Coders**.

Auditors want to stop fraud but auditing is expensive.
Coders want to maximize revenue but don't want to get caught.

The result is a "Mixed Strategy Equilibrium."
Random audits. Occasional gaming. A constant, expensive cycle of checking and hiding. 🔄

Every dollar spent on this game is a dollar NOT spent on doctors and nurses.

How do we break it?
1️⃣ **Simpler pricing** (fewer loopholes).
2️⃣ **Bundled payments** (value over volume).
3️⃣ **AI Auditing** (low-cost transparency).
