# Negotiation Strategy: The First-Mover Advantage ♟️🥇

Is it better to speak first or last?

In the NHRA, the Commonwealth often speaks first.
"Here is the Medicare rebate. Take it or leave it."

This is **Stackelberg Leadership**.
By moving first, the Leader forces the Follower (States) to adapt to *their* reality.

"If I cut GP funding, the State *has* to boost ED funding to stop people dying. So I'll cut."

It’s ruthless, rational, and efficient for the Leader.
But it creates a skewed system where the Follower constantly cleans up the mess.

The fix? **Joint Decision Making**.
Stop the Leader/Follower dynamic. Decide together.
