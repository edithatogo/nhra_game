# Why hospitals fight over "profitable" patients 🏥💰

We think of public health as a safety net.
But inside the system, hospitals are competing like businesses.

Under **Activity-Based Funding (ABF)**, some patients make money (Surplus). Some lose money (Deficit).

Result? The **Internal Competition Game**.
Big urban hospitals "cherry-pick" the high-volume, high-margin work.
Regional hospitals get left with the complex, expensive, low-margin cases.

The "Equilibrium" is a deficit spiral for the safety net. 📉

To fix it, we need:
1️⃣ **Risk Adjustment** (Make complex cases pay fairly).
2️⃣ **Block Funding** (Protect essential services from market forces).
3️⃣ **Network Balancing** (Share the load).
