1/ Did you know public hospitals compete for patients? 🏥🏃‍♂️

It’s called **Internal LHN Competition**, and it creates winners and losers. 🧵

2/ The Rules:
Under Activity-Based Funding (ABF), hospitals get paid a set price for a service.
Price > Cost = Surplus (Profit). 💰
Cost > Price = Deficit (Loss). 💸

3/ The Incentive:
"Cherry-pick" the winners.
High-volume elective surgery? Yes please. ✅
Complex chronic disease in a rural setting? No thanks. ❌

4/ The Players:
🏥 **Urban LHN**: Has scale, specialists, and choice. Captures the revenue.
🏥 **Regional LHN**: Has to take whoever walks in. Absorbs the pressure.

5/ The Equilibrium:
Urban LHN builds a surplus, buys fancy gear, attracts top staff.
Regional LHN spirals into deficit, struggles to recruit.

It creates a two-tier system INSIDE the public system. 📉

6/ It’s not "bad management" in the regions. It’s a structural disadvantage in the price signal.

7/ The Fix:
✅ **Load Sharing**: Mandate that big hospitals take their share of the "hard" stuff.
✅ **Block Funding**: Don't use ABF for markets that don't work (like rural health).
✅ **Price Adjustment**: Pay more for complexity.
