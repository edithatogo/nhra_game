# Parameterisation notes (v8)

This repo uses **stylised** mappings for:

- ED crowding / crowding indices → mortality risk (as a *relative* proxy)
- Ambulance offload (“ramping”) → adverse outcome risk (as a *relative* proxy)

These mappings are **not** used to claim real-world death counts; they serve as *directional* stress indicators.

Suggested empirical anchors (for narrative and order-of-magnitude checks):

- Ambulance ramping and 30-day mortality (Victorian data): Dawson et al., MJA 2022.
- ED crowding and outcomes: multiple systematic reviews and cohort studies (see `reports/v8_report.html` references).
