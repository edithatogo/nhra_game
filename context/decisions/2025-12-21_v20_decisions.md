# Decisions log — v20 (2025-12-21)

## Scope decision
We keep the model as a **stylised mechanism model** to support governance arguments (incentives, alignment, downstream pressure) rather than attempting a predictive forecast.

## Evidence decision
All empirical inputs must point to **publicly retrievable sources**. Where no credible parameter is available, we require an explicit assumption justification and a plausible range used in sensitivity/scenario analysis.

## NEP interpretation
NEP is treated as an annual **$/NWAU** value that is multiplied by a representative activity weight (NWAU) to form an efficient price proxy. The model uses NEP primarily for scenario comparability rather than ABF accounting.
