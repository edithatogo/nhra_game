# System map (conceptual)

The models represent a simplified causal structure:

- **Upstream levers (Commonwealth):** primary care availability (MBS), aged care capacity, NDIS processes, national pricing (NEP), and capped grants under the NHRA.
- **Interface friction:** definitional disputes ("efficient" vs actual), cost-shifting incentives, discharge delays, and fragmented governance.
- **Downstream risk (States/LHNs):** access block, ambulance offload delay, ED throughput, and clinical risk accumulation.

The repository contains both **diagrammatic representations** (Mermaid/Graphviz/D3) and **mechanism simulations** that export time series for scenario comparison.
