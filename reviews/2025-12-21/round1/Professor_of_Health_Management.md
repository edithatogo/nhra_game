## Summary
Strong governance focus and helpful mechanism diagrams. To be management-ready, the output needs a clearer “what to do on Monday” section and a more polished reporting structure.

2. **Reporting structure**: Current reports read like developer artefacts. Convert to a structured management report with sections: baseline, equilibria, sensitivity, scenarios, recommendations.
3. **Transparency**: Include a reproducibility appendix (how to run, what each script produces).

## Specific recommendations
- Add figure/table numbering, titles, captions, abbreviations.
- Add a brief executive synthesis at the end of each results section.
- Provide a “decision table” mapping interventions → expected direction of change across outcomes.
