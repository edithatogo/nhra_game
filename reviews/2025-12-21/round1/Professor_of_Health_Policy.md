# Simulated Review (Round 1)

**Date:** 21 December 2025

**Version under review:** v15 (0.15.0)

## Summary

The framing around vertical fiscal imbalance and “efficient price vs actual cost” is compelling, but the policy narrative must precisely reflect the IHACPA NEP/NWAU mechanics and the NHRA cap features to avoid credibility hits.

## Major comments

1. **Terminology discipline**: NEP is an annual $/NWAU; avoid wording that implies a per-procedure “price”.
2. **Agreement architecture**: Ensure that Schedule K, cap design, and indexation disputes are described as mechanisms, not merely events. Include a clause map as an appendix.
3. **Policy levers as interventions**: The scenario set should explicitly model policy interventions (pooled funding pilots, integration conditions for UCCs, workforce funding of “middle tier”, discharge/aged care capacity).

## Specific recommendations

- Add time-varying NEP vs input cost growth to operationalise the “efficiency gap” over 2025–2030.
- Expand scenarios to include a “policy package” vs “funding-only” comparison.
- Add a one-page position statement draft consistent with RACMA voice.
