# Simulated Review (Round 1)

**Date:** 21 December 2025

**Version under review:** v15 (0.15.0)

## Summary

This is a useful conceptual and computational framing of NHRA negotiations as interacting strategic “games” that manifest as clinical risk (throughput failure, access block, and ambulance offload delay). The contribution is strongest when it links governance incentives to patient-facing outcomes.

## Major comments

1. **Clinical validity of proxies**: “Risk proxy”, “within-4-hours”, and “ambulance offload delay” are defensible but need clearer mapping to established hospital performance concepts and a stronger description of what constitutes a clinically meaningful change.
2. **External validity**: The stylised discharge/occupancy model should be explicit about what it omits (ICU constraints, elective surgery queues, seasonal respiratory peaks, workforce sickness, private sector displacement).
3. **Interpretability**: Leaders will adopt this only if figures are explainable. Tables and figures need captions and short narratives that connect model movement to operational reality.

## Specific recommendations

- Add a short “Clinical interpretation guide” section to the report with thresholds (e.g., what magnitude of offload change matters).
- Add a sensitivity plot that shows which parameters most influence patient flow outcomes.
- Include a limitations paragraph on where clinical nuance is collapsed into a proxy.
