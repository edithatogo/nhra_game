# Literature Search Results: Game Theory in Policy (2020-2025)

## Primary Methodological Source

**Baez Hernandez, A. (2025).** *Games theory. A valuable instrument in decision-making in public policies.*

- **Findings:** Identifies analytical-synthetic and inductive-deductive approaches as predominant. Notes a gap in specific public policy application despite broad interest.
- **Utility:** Provides the most recent systematic framework for our qualitative mapping study.

## Secondary Application Sources

1. **Policy Coordination Games (2023):** Specific approach to modeling multi-stakeholder coordination in health systems (MDPI).
2. **Institutional Isomorphism & Games:** Identified links between regulatory pressure and strategic "masks" (theatre) in bureaucratic environments.
