# Methodology Validation Report
**Author:** Dylan A Mordaunt

This report validates the selection of key methodological frameworks for the NHRA Game Theory Publication Series.

## 1. Institutional Analysis and Development (IAD) Framework
*   **Source:** Ostrom, E. (2005). *Understanding Institutional Diversity*.
*   **Validation:** The IAD framework is the gold standard for analyzing institutional rules and human behavior. It provides the structured "Action Situation" components (Players, Rules, Outcomes) required for our mapping study.
*   **Application:** Used to code the NHRA legal text into discrete game nodes.

## 2. Policy-Oriented Game Theory
*   **Source:** Baez Hernandez, A. (2025). *Games theory. A valuable instrument in decision-making in public policies*.
*   **Validation:** Provides current (2025) evidence of the analytical-synthetic and inductive-deductive methods in public policy research. It justifies the use of GT as a decision-support tool.
*   **Application:** Grounding the Induction phase of our qualitative study.

## 3. Policy Evaluation using Game Theory
*   **Source:** Hermans et al. (2014). *The usefulness of game theory as a method for policy evaluation*.
*   **Validation:** Specifically addresses the transition from policy text to game models. Validates the "Clean Room" approach by emphasizing the need for structured interpretation of policy rules.
*   **Application:** Justifies the multi-lens review process.

## 4. Empirical Context: NHRA Failures
*   **Source:** Duckett, S. (2021). *Vicious cycles: hospital bed block and the National Health Reform Agreement*.
*   **Validation:** Provides the empirical justification for the "Strategic Gaming" hypothesis within the Australian context.
*   **Application:** Grounding the "Background" and "Results" sections of the manuscripts.
