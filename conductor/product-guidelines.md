# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style & Tone
-   **Primary (MJA):** Academic, rigorous, and precise. Adheres to *Medical Journal of Australia* submission guidelines for Original Articles. Focus on methodologic clarity, limitations, and statistical validity.
-   **Secondary (RACMA):** Executive, action-oriented summaries for policy statements. Synthesizes findings into clear negotiation "asks" without sacrificing accuracy.

## Visual Identity
-   **Academic Standard (Primary):** Figures must meet MJA publication specifications:
    -   High resolution (300+ DPI).
    -   **Vector Formats:** Mandatory use of vector formats (SVG, PDF) for all primary outputs to ensure lossless print quality.
    -   Clear, legible fonts (Arial/Helvetica).
    -   Minimalist, data-ink maximized design.
    -   Accessible color palettes (color-blind friendly).

## Communication Principles
1.  **Radical Transparency:** Every parameter must be traceable to a public source URL or a detailed, justified assumption log.
2.  **Uncertainty Quantification (PSA):** All forecasts must be based on **Probabilistic Sensitivity Analysis (PSA)** and present confidence intervals (e.g., 95% CI). Point estimates must never be presented in isolation.
3.  **Neutrality:** Policy scenarios must be modelled objectively, avoiding partisan language or bias.
4.  **Reproducibility First:** The repository structure and documentation must satisfy the "replication test"—an independent researcher should be able to rebuild the results from scratch.

## Reporting Standards & Checklists
To ensure SOTA quality, the project will strictly adhere to the following international guidelines:
-   **CHEERS 2022:** Consolidated Health Economic Evaluation Reporting Standards.
-   **STRESS:** Strengthening The Reporting of Empirical Simulation Studies.
-   **NCBI/NIH:** Best practices for biomedical research reporting.

## Compliance Enforcement
-   **Checklist Deliverables:** Every manuscript draft and major model release must be accompanied by completed CHEERS 2022 and STRESS checklists.
-   **Evidence Audits:** Regular audits of the parameter registry will be conducted to ensure 100% public source traceability or explicit justification.
