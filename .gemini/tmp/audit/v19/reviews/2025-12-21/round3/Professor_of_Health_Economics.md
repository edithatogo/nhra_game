# Simulated peer review — Round 3 (v18) — 2025-12-21


## Overall assessment
The NEP/NWAU clarification is important: NEP is an annual $/NWAU price applied against weights. The v18 drift implementation aligns with that concept by treating NEP and costs as indices over time rather than a single ‘price’.

## Major comments
1. **Model reporting standard**: Consider STRESS (simulation studies) and CHEERS 2022 as the reporting anchors; the manuscript should explicitly state which checklist is followed and provide a supplementary checklist.
2. **Calibration narrative**: Provide a clear table of parameter ranges with short justifications. Do not over-calibrate to IHACPA calculators unless the model is going to use real NWAU logic end-to-end.
3. **Uncertainty analysis**: One-way sensitivity is good; add at least one probabilistic sensitivity analysis (PSA) drawing parameters from plausible distributions.

## Minor comments
- Ensure NEP growth and cost growth values are expressed as annual rates and interpreted consistently.
- If you keep a “cap”, explain whether it is nominal or real and how it interacts with indexation.

## Recommendation
**Major revision**, with a strong path to acceptance if reporting and uncertainty analysis are strengthened.
