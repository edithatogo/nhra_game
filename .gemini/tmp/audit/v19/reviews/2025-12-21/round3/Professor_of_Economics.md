# Simulated peer review — Round 3 (v18) — 2025-12-21

## Overall assessment

The manuscript now has a clearer separation between macro drift (indexation vs costs) and micro strategic behaviour. The stage-game equilibrium reporting is useful, but the paper needs stronger economic interpretation.

## Major comments

1. **Welfare and objective functions**: The model reports outcomes (offload, within-4, RR proxy), but does not articulate an explicit social welfare function or government objective. Even a simple weighted welfare index would allow clearer interpretation and policy trade-offs.
2. **Equilibrium selection and dynamics**: Stage-game equilibria do not uniquely determine dynamic paths. You should explain how strategy rules map from equilibria to year-by-year outcomes, and consider reporting robustness to alternative selection (e.g., payoff-dominant vs risk-dominant).
3. **Heterogeneity**: A single representative system is limiting. At minimum, scenario analysis should include a “metro-dominant” and “regional/remote-heavy” parameterisation.

## Minor comments

- Consider including a discount factor or explicit time preference if claiming intertemporal incentives.

## Recommendation

**Major revision**. The model is interesting but requires clearer economic interpretation.
