# Simulated peer review — Round 3 (v18) — 2025-12-21

## Overall assessment

Explicit modelling of NEP ($/NWAU) and input cost drift is an important methodological upgrade. The intervention scenarios now include recognisable levers (indexation, cost containment, pooled funding, integration), which strengthens policy relevance.

## Major comments

1. **Policy interpretability**: Make it explicit which interventions correspond to *Commonwealth levers* versus *State levers* versus *shared governance*. A table should label each lever and the plausible implementation pathway.
2. **Assumption discipline**: The model necessarily simplifies the NHRA. The manuscript should make a stronger distinction between (a) describing incentives and (b) asserting what governments “will” do.
3. **Equity framing**: Rurality is handled as a risk proxy and valuation ratio. Provide a clearer explanation of why rurality matters here (cost structure, workforce, travel time, limited substitutes).

## Minor comments

- Be consistent in language: “valuation divergence” vs “efficiency gap”.
- Provide a concise description of the cap mechanism and its policy analogue.

## Recommendation

**Revise and resubmit**. Stronger than earlier rounds, particularly with macro drift.
