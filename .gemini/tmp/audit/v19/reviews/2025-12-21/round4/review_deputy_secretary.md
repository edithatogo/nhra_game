# Round 4 peer-review simulation (v19)

Date: 2025-12-21

## Reviewer: Deputy Secretary, Australian Commonwealth Department of Health

### Overall recommendation

**Useful internal briefing tool.** As long as it is framed correctly, it could support structured discussions with states.

### Major points

1. **Use-case clarity.** The model is most useful for exploring levers and trade-offs and for communicating that interface reforms alter incentives; it should not be presented as predicting negotiated shares.
2. **NEP messaging.** The NEP clarification is essential. In briefing products, keep NEP as an annual parameter and avoid quoting single “prices” without context.
3. **Package framing.** The “integration package” and “macro alignment package” mirror the kinds of combined policy moves that are feasible (governance + demand relief + price realism). That framing will resonate.

### Minor points

- Consider adding a “briefing slide” style one-page summary with three key takeaways and the main figure.
- Avoid attributing intent; describe incentives and constraints.

### Suggested edits

- Add a short paragraph on what evidence would be needed to parameterise this more tightly (e.g., time series of NEP, input cost indices, discharge capacity measures).
