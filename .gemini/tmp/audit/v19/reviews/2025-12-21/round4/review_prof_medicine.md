# Round 4 peer-review simulation (v19)

Date: 2025-12-21

## Reviewer: Professor of Medicine

### Overall recommendation

**Minor revision.** The v19 report is clearer about what is and is not clinically interpretable.

### Major points

1. **Clinical meaning of “RR”.** The risk outcome is appropriately treated as a *proxy index* (dimensionless). Please keep the narrative anchored in “relative worsening/improvement” rather than implying absolute event rates.
2. **Pathways are plausible.** The chain *pressure → discharge delay/offload → ED≤4h → risk* mirrors operational experience, and is suitable for a conceptual model.
3. **Avoid false precision.** The model should not be interpreted as forecasting a specific 2030 offload time. It is valuable for *comparative* scenarios and *equilibrium selection* narratives.

### Minor points

- In the baseline table, consider adding one sentence stating that variables are normalised indices or stylised approximations (pressure, NEP, costs).
- Where you mention “safety”, avoid over-claiming: “risk proxy” or “safety proxy” is safer language.

### Suggested edits

- Add one paragraph in the introduction clarifying: “This model supports governance discussions; it does not estimate clinical incident rates.”
