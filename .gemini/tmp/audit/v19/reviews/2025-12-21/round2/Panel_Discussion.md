## Shared priorities
All reviewers agree the model is most useful as an *explanatory* device that makes incentives and regime shifts explicit. The panel’s common concern is that readers may misinterpret outputs as forecasts unless the report repeatedly anchors interpretation.

## Points of tension
- **Precision vs communication:** Economics reviewers wanted more equilibrium transparency and sensitivity; clinical/policy reviewers emphasised decision-relevance and narrative clarity.
- **Governance vs funding messaging:** The Deputy Secretary emphasised balanced language and actionable levers; health policy reviewers pushed for explicit “asks”.

## Ranked priorities for v17
1. **Decision-oriented intervention scenarios** with deltas vs baseline.
2. **Clear figure/table conventions** with abbreviations and section introductions.
3. **Equilibrium selection sensitivity** and continued equilibria exports.
4. **Explicit proxy framing** and limitations.

## Resolution
